Our Full-Cycle Re-Parole for Ukraine Services are designed to help Ukrainian nationals extend their lawful stay in the United States through re-parole. Whether you are applying for re-parole for the first time or renewing your current parole period, we guide you through each step to ensure your status remains valid and uninterrupted.

Document Review & Preparation
We assist you with gathering all necessary documents for your re-parole application, including:
1. Proof of identity (passport, prior immigration documents)
2. Copy of your current parole approval and I-94 record
3. Evidence of continued eligibility for humanitarian parole
4. Supporting documents demonstrating need for re-parole (personal statement, country conditions, etc.)
Our team ensures all documents are properly prepared and meet USCIS requirements.

Filing Re-Parole Application (Form I-131)
Once your documents are ready, we will:
1. Complete and file Form I-131 (Application for Travel Document) for re-parole
2. Ensure the application is accurate and submitted to the correct USCIS location
3. Include all required supporting evidence to strengthen your case
4. Translate all required documents and supporting evidence at no additional cost
5. Take an individual approach to each case, including up-to-date research based on current country conditions and USCIS practices
6. Prepare a customized, case-specific evidence package tailored to your unique situation

Filing Work Authorization (Form I-765)
If you wish to continue working in the U.S., we will:
1. Prepare and file Form I-765 (Application for Employment Authorization) together with your Form I-131 re-parole application
2. Ensure your work authorization is renewed without gaps
3. Include all required supporting documents to strengthen your application and approval chances
4. Timely Filing to Maintain Status:
5. We ensure your re-parole application is filed before your current parole expires to avoid any unlawful presence or gaps in status. Our team tracks deadlines and processing timelines closely.

Follow-Up with USCIS & Request for Evidence (If Needed)
After submission, we monitor your case status and:
1. Respond to any Requests for Evidence (RFE)
2. Communicate with USCIS as needed
3. Keep you informed throughout the process 

Re-Parole Approval & Next Steps
Once your re-parole is approved, we:
1. Notify you immediately
2. Explain your updated status and validity period
3. Provide guidance on maintaining compliance and future renewals
4. Assist you with completing any remaining steps required by USCIS, including guidance on how to pay any outstanding fees referenced in a conditional approval notice to obtain final re-parole approval

Post-Approval Support
We continue to support you after approval by:
1. Assisting with future re-parole extensions
2. Advising on travel, employment, and status compliance

Expedited Processing Request (Separate Service)
For applications that have already been submitted and are pending with USCIS for an extended period, we offer expedited processing assistance as a separate service for an additional fee.
This service includes:
1. Preparing and submitting a formal expedite request to USCIS
2. Gathering and organizing supporting evidence (financial hardship, urgent humanitarian reasons, etc.)
3. Drafting a strong, case-specific explanation to support expedited review
4. Following up with USCIS regarding the status of the expedite request
