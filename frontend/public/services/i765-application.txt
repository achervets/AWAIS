Our Full-Cycle I-765 Initial Application and Renewal Service is designed to guide individuals through the process of obtaining and renewing Employment Authorization (work permit) in the United States. Whether you're applying for a work permit for the first time or need to renew your current authorization, we provide a comprehensive, step-by-step approach to ensure your application is processed smoothly and efficiently.

Document Review & Preparation
We assist you with gathering all necessary documents required for your I-765 application. These may include:
1. Proof of identity (e.g., passport, birth certificate, government-issued ID)
2. Evidence of your current immigration status
3. Any prior employment authorization documents (if applicable)
4. Additional supporting documents based on your eligibility category
Our team ensures that all documents are properly formatted and compliant with USCIS requirements.

Filing Initial I-765 Application
For first-time applicants, we will:
1. Complete and file Form I-765 (Application for Employment Authorization) on your behalf, ensuring it is filled out correctly and submitted to the appropriate USCIS office.
2. Provide guidance on the necessary filing fees, as well as any required supporting documents to include with your application.

Filing I-765 Renewal Application
For applicants seeking to renew their work authorization, we will:
1. Complete and file your I-765 renewal application, ensuring all necessary documentation is included and that your application is submitted in a timely manner to avoid any lapses in employment authorization.
2. Track the expiration date of your current authorization and ensure that your renewal application is filed within the proper time frame to prevent delays.

Timely Filing to Avoid Status Issues
We ensure that your I-765 application, whether initial or renewal, is filed well before your current work permit expires (or before you wish to begin work if applying for the first time). This helps avoid gaps in employment authorization and ensures compliance with U.S. immigration regulations.

Follow-Up with USCIS & Request for Additional Evidence (If Needed)
Once your I-765 application is submitted, we track the status and prepare and submit a Request for Evidence (RFE), if requested. 

Approval Notification & Employment Authorization Card (EAD)
Upon approval, we will notify you immediately and guide you through the process of receiving your Employment Authorization Card (EAD). We also provide instructions on how to begin working legally in the U.S. once your card is issued.

Post-Approval Support
We continue to offer support after your I-765 application is approved, assisting with any further requirements, such as employer verification or questions about your employment status. Additionally, we help you track the expiration of your EAD card and prepare you for any future renewals.

Renewal of Employment Authorization
To avoid lapses in employment authorization, we assist in monitoring your EAD card’s expiration date. Our team ensures your I-765 renewal application is filed on time, helping you stay on top of your work status and preventing any disruption in employment.
