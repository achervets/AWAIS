Our Full-Cycle SB-1 (Returning Resident) Visa Services are designed to help lawful permanent residents who remained outside of the United States for an extended period without obtaining a Reentry Permit to return and restore their status. We guide you through the entire process - from eligibility assessment to consular approval - ensuring your case is properly prepared and supported.

Case Strategy & Eligibility Assessment
1. We begin with a detailed review of your situation to determine eligibility for SB-1 status.
2. Evaluate your travel history and reasons for extended absence
3. Analyze whether your stay abroad was beyond your control
4. Identify potential risks or issues in advance
5. Provide a tailored strategy for your specific case

Document Review & Preparation
We assist in collecting and preparing all required documents, including:
1. Proof of lawful permanent resident status 
2. Evidence of ties to the U.S. 
3. Documentation explaining extended stay abroad
4. Civil documents
We translate all required documents and supporting evidence at no additional cost.

Individualized Case Preparation
Each case is handled with an individual approach, including:
1. Up-to-date research based on current consular practices
2. Careful review of evidence to demonstrate continuous intent to return to the U.S.
3. Preparation of a strong, well-organized SB-1 application package

Filing SB-1 Application and Interview (Form DS-117)
We will:
1. Prepare and assist with submission of Form DS-117 (Application to Determine Returning Resident Status)
2. Ensure all forms are completed accurately and submitted to the appropriate U.S. Embassy or Consulate
3. Prepare a customized, case-specific evidence package tailored to your situation
4. Prepare you for the SB-1 interview at the U.S. Embassy or Consulate
5. Prepare a complete, well-organized document package in the correct order for each client, so you can simply print it and bring it with you to your interview in the U.S. Embassy 
6. Assist with any additional requests from the consulate

Approval & Next Steps
Once your SB-1 visa is approved, we:
1. Notify you immediately
2. Provide instructions for travel and re-entry into the U.S.
3. Guide you on maintaining your permanent resident status moving forward 

Post-Entry Guidance (U.S. Arrival)
After returning to the U.S., we provide guidance on:
1. Maintaining lawful permanent resident status
2. Avoiding future travel-related issues
