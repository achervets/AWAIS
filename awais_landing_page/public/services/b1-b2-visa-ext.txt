Our Full-Cycle B1/B2 Visa Status Extension Services are designed to provide individuals currently on a B1/B2 visa with a comprehensive, step-by-step approach to extending their stay in the United States. 
This service is specifically for people who are on a B1/B2 visa and wish to legally extend their stay in the U.S. under B1/B2 visa for an additional 180 days from the Admit Until Date in their I-94 form. From the initial consultation to visa approval, we ensure every part of the process is handled professionally and efficiently.
Document Review & Preparation:
We assist you with gathering all necessary documents for your status extension application, including your passport, I-94 form, proof of finances, personal statement, trip plan, and any other required materials. Our team ensures all your documents are properly formatted and compliant with USCIS requirements.
Filing I-539 (Application to Extend/Change Nonimmigrant Status):
Once your documents are in order, we will:
Complete and file the I-539 form—the primary application for extending your stay. Our team ensures the form is filled out correctly and submitted to the right USCIS office in a timely manner.
Ensure that all supplementary documentation, including financial proof and any additional supporting forms, is properly included with your application to avoid delays.
Assisting with I-134 Affidavit of Support (If Needed):
If additional financial proof is required, we will:
Prepare the I-134 Affidavit of Support form, demonstrating that you have a sponsor (family or friend) willing to support your stay in the U.S. financially if needed.
Guide you in gathering all relevant documentation for your financial support, ensuring it meets USCIS requirements.
Timely Filing to Avoid Overstay:
We ensure that your extension application is filed well before your current stay expires to avoid any gaps in your legal status. We will monitor the expiration date of your current visa and ensure timely filing to keep you compliant with U.S. immigration laws.
Follow-Up with USCIS & Request for Additional Evidence (If Needed):
After your application is submitted, we track the status and prepare and submit a Request for Evidence (RFE), if requested. 
Approval Notification & Next Steps:
Once your extension is approved, we notify you immediately and provide guidance on the next steps, including confirming the new "Admit Until Date" in your I-94 form. We ensure your stay remains compliant with U.S. immigration laws.
Post-Extension Support:
We provide ongoing support throughout the extension process, answering any questions or addressing concerns you may have. If you need to take further steps, such as adjusting your status or preparing for a visa renewal, we are here to guide you.
Visa Extension Delivery:
Once your extension is approved, we ensure that your updated status and I-94 information are correctly processed and delivered to you. We help you understand the updated details of your stay, ensuring you remain in compliance with U.S. immigration requirements.
