Our Full-Cycle Change of Status Services are designed to assist individuals who are currently in the U.S. on a non-immigrant visa and wish to change their status to a different visa category. Whether you're looking to transition from a B1/B2 visa to an F1 student visa, or H1B work visa to B1/B2 visa, or any other visa type, we offer a comprehensive, step-by-step approach to navigating the change of status process. From the initial consultation to approval, we ensure that every part of your application is handled professionally and efficiently.

Document Review and Preparation
We assist you in gathering all required documents for your change of status application. This includes your passport, I-94, proof of financial support, and any additional materials specific to the new visa type. Our team ensures that all documents are properly formatted and meet the necessary requirements for USCIS submission.

Filing the Application (Form I-539 or Relevant Forms)
Once all documents are in order, we will:
1. Complete and file the appropriate forms, such as Form I-539 (Application to Extend/Change Nonimmigrant Status), or any other relevant forms for your specific visa category.
2. Ensure that the forms are filled out correctly and submitted to the right USCIS office in a timely manner.

Assisting with I-134 Affidavit of Support (If Needed)
If additional financial proof is required, we will:
1. Prepare the I-134 Affidavit of Support form, demonstrating that you have a sponsor (family or friend) willing to support your stay in the U.S. financially if needed.
2. Guide you in gathering all relevant documentation for your financial support, ensuring it meets USCIS requirements.

Assisting with Required Supplementary Forms (If Applicable)
For certain visa types, additional forms may be required. We will assist in preparing:
1. Form I-20 for students (if transitioning to an F1 visa), or
2. Any other required supplementary forms based on the visa type you're changing to.

Timely Filing to Avoid Status Issues:
We ensure that your application is filed well before your current visa expires to avoid any gaps in your legal status. Our team carefully monitors your visa expiration date to ensure timely filing, keeping you compliant with U.S. immigration laws.

Follow-Up with USCIS & Request for Additional Evidence (If Needed)
After your application is submitted, we track the status and prepare and submit a Request for Evidence (RFE), if requested. 

Approval Notification & Next Steps
Once your change of status application is approved, we notify you immediately and provide guidance on the next steps, such as applying for a visa stamp at a U.S. consulate or adjusting your status within the U.S.

Post-Approval Support
We offer ongoing support throughout the change of status process, addressing any concerns or questions you may have. If your situation requires further steps, such as applying for a visa extension or adjustment of status, we’re here to help.

Visa Status Delivery & Next Steps
Once your new status is approved, we ensure you receive all necessary documentation and explain the updated details of your status, ensuring you remain compliant with U.S. immigration laws.
