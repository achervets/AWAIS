Our Full-Cycle K Visa Services are designed to guide U.S. citizens and their foreign fiancé(e)s or spouses through the entire immigration process - from initial petition to arrival in the United States and beyond. We handle each case individually, ensuring accuracy, efficiency, and full compliance with USCIS and Department of State requirements.

Case Strategy & Eligibility Assessment
1. We begin with a detailed review of your situation to determine eligibility and the best pathway (K-1 fiancé(e) visa or K-3 spouse visa).
2. Evaluate relationship history and documentation
3. Identify potential risks or issues in advance
4. Provide a tailored strategy for your specific case 

Document Review & Preparation
We assist in collecting and preparing all required documents, including:
1. Proof of U.S. citizenship (petitioner)
2. Evidence of a bona fide relationship
3. Proof of in-person meeting (if applicable)
4. Civil documents 
We translate all required documents and supporting evidence at no additional cost.
Throughout the process, we guide you step-by-step, ensuring you understand each stage and are fully prepared from start to finish.

Individualized Case Preparation
Each case is handled with an individual approach, including:
1. Up-to-date research based on current USCIS and consular practices
2. Careful review of relationship evidence to meet scrutiny standards
3. Preparation of a strong, well-organized petition package

Filing Petition with USCIS (Form I-129F)
We will:
1. Prepare and file Form I-129F 
2. Ensure all forms are completed accurately and submitted to the correct USCIS office
3. Prepare a customized, case-specific evidence package tailored to your relationship

Follow-Up & USCIS Communication
We monitor your case throughout the process and:
1. Respond to Requests for Evidence (RFEs), if issued
2. Track case progress and provide updates
3. Ensure all deadlines are met

Consular Processing Guidance
Once the petition is approved and forwarded to the National Visa Center (NVC) and U.S. consulate, we will:
1. Guide you through DS-160/DS-260 completion (as applicable)
2. Assist with scheduling the visa interview
3. Prepare you for the consular interview with detailed guidance and expectations
4. Review all required documents for the interview stage
5. Prepare a complete, well-organized document package in the correct order for each client, so you can simply print it and bring it with you to your interview in the U.S. Embassy.

K-2 / K-4 Dependent Visa Support
For accompanying children, we:
1. Prepare and include all required documentation
2. Ensure proper filing and coordination with the principal applicant’s case
4. Guide through the same consular process for dependents 

Approval & Next Steps
Once your visa is approved, we:
1. Notify you immediately
2. Provide instructions for entry into the U.S.
3. Guide you through post-entry immigration steps

Post-Entry Guidance (U.S. Arrival)
After entering the U.S., we provide guidance on next steps:
1. For K-1 visa holders: assistance with marriage timeline requirements and next steps
