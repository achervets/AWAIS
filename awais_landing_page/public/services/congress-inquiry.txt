For cases experiencing delays, issues, or lack of response from USCIS, we offer assistance with preparing a formal inquiry to your local Congressman or Congressional office.
This service includes:
1. Drafting a detailed, case-specific letter outlining your situation and concerns
2. Presenting your case clearly and professionally to request assistance or intervention
3. Identifying the appropriate Congressional office based on your location
4. Completing the required Congressional inquiry forms on your behalf
5. Assisting with submission and providing guidance on next steps
6. Supporting you with any follow-up communication with the Congressman’s office
