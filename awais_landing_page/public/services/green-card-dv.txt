If you have been selected in the DV Lottery, we provide full assistance throughout the process of securing your Green Card, whether you're currently in the U.S. or abroad.

For Applicants Already in the U.S.:
Adjustment of Status (Form I-485): 
If you're in the U.S. and win the DV Lottery, we help you file Form I-485 (Application to Adjust Status) to change your status to a permanent resident without needing to leave the country.
We ensure all the necessary documents, including your DV Lottery confirmation number, photos, medical exam, and other required materials, are properly prepared.
We take care of the completing and the timely submission of your Form I-485 and any supplementary forms, such as Form I-864 (Affidavit of Support).
Our team will guide you through your biometrics appointment, medical exam, and any interviews that may be part of the process.
Employment Authorization (Form I-765): If you're looking to work while waiting for your Green Card, we are applying for work authorization for you, allowing you to work legally in the U.S. during the process.
Travel Authorization (Form I-131): If you need to travel outside the U.S. while your Green Card application is pending, we will file an Advance Parole for you, using Form I-131, so you can return without interrupting your Green Card application.

For Applicants Outside the U.S.:
Consular Processing (Form DS-260): If you're outside the U.S., we complete Form DS-260 (Immigrant Visa and Alien Registration Application) to begin the process of obtaining your Green Card through consular processing at the U.S. embassy or consulate of your convenience.
We assist in gathering all required documents and prepare them for you, for your visa interview.
We provide guidance on scheduling your medical exam and securing the necessary police certificates for the interview.
We help you prepare for your interview, ensuring you have everything you need for a smooth process.
Form DS-260 Submission and Visa Fee Payment: We manage the completion and submission of Form DS-260 and handle the visa application fee payments on your behalf.
Visa Approval & U.S. Entry: Once your immigrant visa is approved, we guide you through obtaining your U.S. Green Card by working with the embassy for your visa stamp and ensuring your entry into the U.S. through the port of entry.
