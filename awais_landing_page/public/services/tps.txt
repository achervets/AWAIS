Our Full-Cycle TPS (Temporary Protected Status) Services are designed to provide individuals from designated countries with a comprehensive, step-by-step approach to obtaining and maintaining Temporary Protected Status in the United States. Whether you are applying for TPS for the first time or renewing your status, we ensure the process is handled professionally and efficiently, guiding you through each stage to ensure your stay in the U.S. remains legal and protected.
Document Review & Preparation:
We assist you with gathering all necessary documents for your TPS application, including:
Proof of identity (e.g., passport, birth certificate)
Proof of nationality and entry into the U.S.
Evidence of continuous residence and physical presence in the U.S.
Any other required documentation specific to your situation
Our team ensures that all documents are properly formatted and meet USCIS requirements.
Filing TPS Application (Form I-821):
Once your documents are in order, we will:
Complete and file Form I-821 (Application for Temporary Protected Status), the primary form for obtaining TPS.
Ensure the form is filled out correctly and submitted to the correct USCIS office in a timely manner, avoiding any delays in processing.
Filing Work Authorization (Form I-765):
For applicants who wish to obtain work authorization while under TPS, we will:
Help you complete Form I-765 (Application for Employment Authorization), allowing you to work legally in the U.S. while your TPS is valid.
Guide you through the process of submitting the form and supporting documents.
Filing Travel Authorization (Form I-131) (If Applicable):
If you wish to travel outside the U.S. while under TPS, we will assist with the application for Advance Parole through Form I-131 (Application for Travel Document). This form allows you to travel and return to the U.S. without losing your TPS status.
Timely Filing to Avoid Status Issues:
We ensure that your application is filed within the designated TPS registration period, avoiding any lapses in status. Our team keeps track of deadlines and USCIS processing times to ensure timely submission.
Follow-Up with USCIS & Request for Additional Evidence (If Needed):
After your application is submitted, we track the status and prepare and submit a Request for Evidence (RFE), if requested. 
TPS Approval Notification & Next Steps:
Once your TPS application is approved, we notify you immediately and explain the next steps. This includes guidance on obtaining work authorization, traveling with TPS, or extending your status if eligible.
Post-Approval Support:
We continue to support you throughout your TPS status. If your TPS needs to be renewed or extended, we will assist in gathering the necessary documents and submitting your renewal application on time, ensuring there is no lapse in your status.
Renewal of TPS:
TPS is not permanent and requires periodic renewals. We help you keep track of your TPS expiration date and assist in filing for the renewal application before your status expires, so you can maintain continuous protected status.
