Our Full-Cycle I-131 Application Services are designed to guide individuals through the process of applying for or renewing Advance Parole, a Reentry Permit, or a Refugee Travel Document under Form I-131 (Application for Travel Document). Whether you need to travel outside the U.S. temporarily without jeopardizing your immigration status or need to apply for a reentry permit, we provide a comprehensive, step-by-step approach to ensure your application is processed smoothly and efficiently
Document Review & Preparation:
We assist you with gathering the necessary documents required for your I-131 application, including:
Proof of identity (e.g., passport, birth certificate)
Evidence of your current immigration status in the U.S.
Documentation supporting your eligibility for the specific type of travel document (e.g., proof of refugee status, permanent residency, or other status)
Any additional documents required based on your specific case
Our team ensures that all documents are correctly formatted and compliant with USCIS requirements.
Filing Advance Parole (Form I-131) for Temporary Travel:
For applicants seeking to travel outside the U.S. temporarily without losing their current status, we will:
Complete and file Form I-131 for Advance Parole, allowing you to return to the U.S. after travel without abandoning your immigration status.
Ensure that all necessary documentation and information are included with the application, making sure the process is as smooth and timely as possible.
Filing Reentry Permit (Form I-131):
For lawful permanent residents (LPRs) who need to travel outside the U.S. for extended periods of time, we will:
Complete and file Form I-131 for a Reentry Permit, allowing you to maintain your permanent resident status while outside the U.S. for extended durations.
Ensure that the necessary supporting documents, such as proof of permanent residency, are included to ensure the application is accepted and processed without issues.
Filing Refugee Travel Document (Form I-131):
For individuals who have refugee or asylum status in the U.S., we will:
Assist in completing and filing Form I-131 to obtain a Refugee Travel Document. This document allows you to travel outside the U.S. and return without losing your refugee or asylum status.
Help gather any additional supporting documents, such as your refugee status approval or other documentation required for the application.
Timely Filing to Avoid Status Issues:
We ensure that your I-131 application is filed with adequate time before your planned travel. Our team helps you avoid any gaps or issues with your status while you’re away from the U.S., ensuring you can return safely without complications.
Follow-Up with USCIS & Request for Additional Evidence (If Needed):
Once your I-131 application is submitted, we track the status and prepare and submit a Request for Evidence (RFE), if requested. 
Approval Notification & Travel Document Receipt:
Upon approval, we will notify you immediately and explain the next steps. Once the travel document is issued, we guide you through the process of using it for your planned travel, ensuring you understand how to reenter the U.S. or maintain your status while abroad.
Post-Approval Support:
We continue to offer support after your I-131 application is approved, assisting with any follow-up actions, such as explaining travel restrictions, reentry processes, or future renewals. Our team ensures you are fully prepared for your travels and reentry into the U.S.
