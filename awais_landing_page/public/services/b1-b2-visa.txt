What is the I-134 Form and Why is it Needed?
The I-134 is a form used to demonstrate that you have sufficient financial support for your trip to the United States, particularly when you do not have enough funds in your bank accounts to cover the expenses of your visit. The form acts as a guarantee from a U.S. sponsor (such as a friend, relative, or business associate) that they will provide financial support during your stay in the U.S. The I-134 is required in cases where the applicant’s financial resources do not meet the U.S. embassy’s requirements. It strengthens your visa application by reassuring the consulate that you will not be a financial burden while in the U.S.
