Our Full-Cycle F1 Visa Services are designed to provide students with a comprehensive, step-by-step approach to successfully obtaining a student visa for studying in the United States. From the initial consultation to visa approval, we ensure every part of the process is handled professionally and efficiently.

University Admission & SEVIS Registration Assistance
We assist you with understanding the required documentation for your university application, including support in securing the I-20 form from the institution and help with the SEVIS (Student and Exchange Visitor Information System) registration process, which is mandatory for all F1 applicants.

University Search & Scholarship Assistance (Additional Cost)
For an additional fee, we can help you search for universities that align with your academic and career goals. Our team also helps you find and apply for academic and/or athletic scholarships that can help cover tuition and living expenses while studying in the U.S.

Document Preparation
We provide guidance on gathering all necessary documents for your F1 visa application, including academic transcripts, standardized test scores (e.g., SAT, TOEFL, IELTS, GRE), financial proof (e.g., bank statements, affidavits of support), and the I-20 form. We assist in creating a detailed list of documents and compile a Masterfile, which includes a comprehensive list of required and additional documents, copies, and certified translations (if applicable). Our team ensures all your documents are properly formatted and compliant with U.S. embassy requirements.

Test Registration Assistance
We help you with registering and scheduling all required tests such as SAT, TOEFL, or any other exams necessary for your application. Additionally, we assist in paying the test fees and provide guidance on the best sources for test preparation.

Visa Application (DS-160) & Interview Date Search
While your documents are being prepared, we draft and submit your DS-160 form—the primary visa application form. Simultaneously, we help you schedule an interview date at the nearest U.S. consulate, ensuring it is as early as possible for your travel plans.

Interview Date Optimization & Research
We don’t just stop at submitting your application—we actively seek the earliest possible interview dates for you at the softest U.S. embassy that is also convenient for you to get to. We research and gather statistics about U.S. embassies known for being more lenient in visa approvals, ensuring that your application is presented in the best possible light.

Financial Support & I-134 Form (If Needed)
In cases where additional financial proof is required, we help prepare the I-134 Affidavit of Support form. This form demonstrates that you have a sponsor (family or friend) willing to support your stay in the U.S. financially if needed. We guide you in gathering all relevant documentation for your financial support.

Visa Interview Preparation
We help you prepare for your F1 visa interview by conducting mock interviews, providing tailored advice on answering common interview questions, and ensuring you understand the key points you need to convey to the consular officer.

Post-Interview Support
After the interview, we continue to assist you with any follow-up actions, ensuring the smooth processing of your visa. We help with additional requirements and provide guidance in case of any delays or complications.

Visa Delivery
Once your F1 visa is approved, we ensure you receive it promptly and assist with any necessary arrangements for the collection or delivery of your visa and related documents.
